Privacy

This site keeps nothing.

Last updated 2026-10-07

No cookies, no tracking, no analytics

homeostat.so is a static website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, loads no third-party scripts, fonts or images, and contains no forms. The fonts on this page are served from this domain. We do not know who visits, and we have built nothing to find out.

The hosting provider that serves these files may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address, time and requested path) for operational purposes, under its own policy. We do not add anything to that.

Contact by email

The only way to reach us from this site is the hello@homeostat.so links, which open your own email client. Nothing is submitted through the website itself. If you write to us, we keep your message and address in our mailbox so we can reply and, if you asked for it, tell you when Homeostat is ready to install. We do not share your address with anyone, and you can ask us to delete it at any time by replying to any message.

About the product

Homeostat, the software, is self-hosted: it runs on your own machine and stores your health data in a SQLite file there. It has no account system and no cloud service of ours behind it. Whether any of your data is sent to a remote AI engine is a setting you turn on per engine, and it is off by default. The software's own privacy behaviour is documented with the software.
